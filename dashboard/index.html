How the model works (radar-3)

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × tenure multiplier × chain multiplier. Every number comes from this snapshot — no protocol name is ever special-cased.

  • Category multiple. The live-token protocols in the same category, each one's mcap ÷ TVL; the trimmed median (middle half, top and bottom quartiles dropped), shrunk toward the global median by peer count — weight n ÷ (n + 3) — and capped at 3.00×. The cap is high enough that a genuinely dear category still prices far above a cheap one, but no single peer's extreme ratio becomes the multiple. A category with no live peers takes the global median. The table shows every category's peer count and peer range, so any multiple can be audited back to the snapshot.
  • Growth multiplier. The protocol's 7-day TVL change relative to its category's median 7-day change (clamped to −60%…+120% first): 1 + 0.50 × (change − category median) ÷ 100, clamped to 0.50…1.60.
  • Tenure multiplier. A hump in age: 0.85 at listing, rising to 1.20 at 730 days of running with no token, then gently falling back toward 1.00 for very old no-token protocols.
  • Chain multiplier. 1 + 0.10 × ln(chains) ÷ ln(16), capped at 1.10 — a small boost for protocols live on more chains.
